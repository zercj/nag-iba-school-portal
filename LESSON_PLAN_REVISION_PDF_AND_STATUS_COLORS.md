# Lesson-plan revision PDF and status display update

- Administrators can choose Approved or For Revision for a submission awaiting review.
- For Revision requires an administrator-uploaded PDF (maximum 8 MB). The PDF is stored with the submission and can be downloaded by the assigned teacher.
- The teacher uploads a revised lesson-plan PDF; the previous lesson-plan version and associated revision instructions are retained in revision history.
- Overdue and late items keep their amber highlight. Completed on-time submissions are green; completed late submissions use a subtle yellow highlight.
- The admin progress tables and task details are hidden until a term is selected. Select a term to display that term's tasks, including overdue, late, approved, and completed statuses.
- Existing deployments are migrated by the startup schema script (`schema.sql`).
